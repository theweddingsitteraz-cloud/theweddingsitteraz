Services Offered

Morning-of Care

Transport & Ceremony Care

FAQs

Good Questions Deserve Good Answers

Don't see what you're looking for? Reach out — we're happy to chat through any special requests.

Email Us
How does the meet and greet work?+
We arrange a meet and greet following the submission of your intake form to further discuss details and ensure your pups know us before the big day.
Do you care for animals other than dogs?+
We currently only provide services for dogs, but if you have a special request please send it our way!
What does the day-of schedule look like?+
We provide a walk or play time prior to transporting your VIP guests to the wedding venue, as well as upon return. We typically arrive at the venue 30 minutes prior to ceremony time — but include any special timeline requests in your intake form!
Can you serve dinner or give medications?+
Absolutely. We are happy to serve dinner or administer medications if needed — just include that information in your intake form.
Will we get photos of our dog on our wedding day?+
Yes! We snap photos of your good boy or girl (or both!) throughout the day so you can look back at their wedding day experience. Honestly, one of the best parts if you ask us.
How is pricing determined?+
Pricing varies based on desired hours of service, distance to the wedding venue, and number of dogs. Reach out and we'll put together the right package for your day.
